# Codec Module Specification

**Module:** TLV320AIC3104 audio codec, self-contained on hierarchical sheet `codec.kicad_sch`
**Scope:** The codec and every component required to make it work as an I²S + I²C peripheral for a Raspberry Pi Zero 2 W, driving a mono handset earpiece and accepting mono handset mic audio. Nothing outside this module.
**Status:** Draft — awaiting user sign-off before implementation (Phase 2 Task 2.2 of `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-04-13-codec-compartmentalized-rebuild.md`)

---

## 1. What this module is responsible for

- Accepting I²S audio data (PCM frames, 48 kHz, S16_LE, stereo capture / mono playback) from the Pi
- Accepting I²C control messages (register read/write for codec setup) from the Pi
- Capturing audio from a mono electret mic in the handset, amplifying and digitising it, emitting it as I²S DOUT to the Pi
- Receiving mono audio from the Pi via I²S DIN, converting to analog, and driving the handset earpiece directly
- Generating its own DVDD (1.8 V digital core supply) from the incoming +3V3 via an onboard LDO
- Decoupling every supply pin per datasheet §13.1

## 2. What this module is NOT responsible for

- Any amplification beyond what the AIC3104's internal BTL headphone driver provides (the 140 Ω earpiece does not need it)
- Ringer audio (the bell is a separate electromechanical path driven by the DRV8871 motor driver — out of the codec signal chain entirely)
- Mic kill-switch logic (the D2F-01F on-cradle switch is in series with the mic line, external to this module; when it opens, the codec sees an open input and digitises silence)
- I²C pullups (the Pi Zero 2 W's internal ~1.8 kΩ pullups on GPIO2/3 are sufficient; no external pullups on this module)
- GPCLK0 generation (that comes from the Pi's GPIO4 configured as ALT0 via dtparam — a software concern, documented below)
- Power sequencing logic (DVDD-last sequencing is provided automatically by the LDO's slower ramp time vs. the already-on +3V3 rail)

## 4. Power architecture

### 4.1 Rails

- **+3V3** — supplied from the parent schematic's main 3.3 V rail (U5 AMS1117-3.3 on the main board). Feeds AVDD, IOVDD, DRVDD×2, and the XC6206 LDO's input.
- **+1V8 (DVDD)** — generated locally on the codec sheet by an XC6206P182MR LDO, fed from +3V3. Powers only the codec's DVDD pin (pin 32) and nothing else. Does NOT leave the sheet.

The +1V8 rail is internal to the sheet only. No sheet port exposes it. This is a key compartmentalization invariant — the checker verifies that the DVDD net has exactly the LDO and the codec pin on it, nothing else.

### 4.2 XC6206P182MR LDO subcircuit

- **U_LDO:** XC6206P182MR, SOT-23-3, LCSC `C347373`
  - Pin 1 VIN → +3V3 rail
  - Pin 2 GND → GND
  - Pin 3 VOUT → +1V8 (DVDD) rail
- **C_LDO_IN:** 100 nF 0402 X7R ceramic, between +3V3 and GND, placed < 1 mm from the LDO VIN pin
- **C_LDO_OUT:** 10 µF 0402 X7R ceramic, between +1V8 and GND, placed < 1 mm from the LDO VOUT pin

XC6206 datasheet requires ≥ 1 µF on output for stability. 10 µF exceeds the requirement and matches both PicoAudioBoard and the datasheet's Fig 11-1 bulk cap recommendation.

### 4.3 DVDD pin decoupling (at U1 pin 32)

- **C_DVDD_1:** 100 nF 0402 X7R, close-in at pin 32, to GND
- **C_DVDD_2:** 1 µF 0402 X7R, also close-in at pin 32 if board area permits; otherwise the LDO output cap C_LDO_OUT serves this role if placement puts it within ~2 mm of pin 32

Total caps on DVDD net: C_LDO_OUT (10 µF) + C_DVDD_1 (100 nF) + optionally C_DVDD_2 (1 µF) = three caps, aligned with datasheet Fig 11-1.

### 4.4 +3V3 decoupling — per-pin close-in caps

One 100 nF 0402 X7R per power pin on the codec, each placed < 1 mm from its pin, each to GND. This was deliberately omitted from PicoAudioBoard but is required by datasheet §13.1 ("decoupling capacitors ... as close as possible to the device power supply terminals") and is present in the Teensy 4 8×8 Audio Board reference. We include it.

- **C_IOVDD:** 100 nF 0402 close-in at pin 7 (IOVDD), to GND
- **C_DRVDD1:** 100 nF 0402 close-in at pin 18 (DRVDD #1), to GND
- **C_DRVDD2:** 100 nF 0402 close-in at pin 24 (DRVDD #2), to GND
- **C_AVDD:** 100 nF 0402 close-in at pin 25 (AVDD), to GND

Plus bulk on the +3V3 rail somewhere in the cluster:
- **C_3V3_BULK_1:** 1 µF 0402 X7R, near U1
- **C_3V3_BULK_2:** 10 µF 0402 or 0603 X7R, near U1 (one bulk cap serving all four +3V3 power pins — acceptable per datasheet when supplemented by the per-pin close-in caps above)

Total +3V3 rail decoupling in the cluster: 4× 100 nF + 1× 1 µF + 1× 10 µF = 6 caps.

### 4.5 No ferrite beads, no LC filters, no RC-isolated rails in this rev

The datasheet allows (but does not mandate) a ferrite bead between DVDD and AVDD to keep digital switching noise out of the analog supply. PicoAudioBoard does not use one. Teensy 4 8×8 does. **Decision: skip the ferrite bead in this rev.** If the first fabricated board shows audible digital hash on the mic path, add a bead as a later rev. This keeps the BOM and the layout simpler now.

## 5. Audio signal topology

### 5.1 Microphone (mono single-ended with differential codec input)

The handset has one electret mic with two wires. The mic is externally biased from MICBIAS through a series resistor, then capacitively coupled into the codec's MIC1L differential input pair.

```
MICBIAS (pin 15) ──[R_BIAS 2.2k]──► MIC_IN port ──► [handset mic "+" terminal]
                                                        │
                                                   [mic element]
                                                        │
                      MIC_RETURN port ◄── [handset mic "-" terminal]
                             │
                             └── local AGND on the codec sheet
```

And the signal tap into the codec:

```
MIC_IN ──[C_MIC_P 0.47µF]──► MIC1LP (pin 10)
                                     │
                            (codec internal PGA)
                                     │
local AGND ──[C_MIC_N 0.47µF]──► MIC1LM (pin 11)
```

- **R_BIAS:** 2.2 kΩ 0402, between MICBIAS (pin 15) and MIC_IN. This is the standard electret bias resistor from the datasheet typical application.
- **C_MIC_P:** 0.47 µF 0402 X7R or C0G, between MIC_IN and MIC1LP (pin 10). 0.47 µF gives a ~3.4 Hz high-pass corner into the codec's nominal 100 kΩ input impedance, well below the speech band (300 Hz and up). Size change vs. PicoAudioBoard's 10 µF: our value is the datasheet typical and rejects subsonic drift.
- **C_MIC_N:** 0.47 µF 0402, between AGND and MIC1LM (pin 11). Provides an AC-coupled common-mode reference to the codec's differential input without DC-shorting the input. This corrects the prior session's "MIC1LM hard-grounded" defect.
- **C_MICBIAS:** 100 nF 0402 X7R, between MICBIAS (pin 15) and AGND, close-in to pin 15. Bypasses the MICBIAS regulator. PicoAudioBoard omits this cap; we add it per datasheet Fig 11-1 and the Teensy reference.

### 5.2 Earpiece (mono BTL from HPLOUT/HPLCOM)

The handset earpiece is a 140 Ω dynamic transducer (measured on the Sangyn Retro 2500 donor phone per `docs/build/wiring.md:184`). The AIC3104's left headphone output pair (HPLOUT + HPLCOM) drives it directly in BTL mode.

```
U1 HPLOUT (pin 19) ──► EAR_P port ──► [earpiece terminal A]
                                              │
                                       [earpiece coil, 140 Ω]
                                              │
U1 HPLCOM (pin 20) ──► EAR_N port ──► [earpiece terminal B]
```

- **No output coupling caps.** BTL mode biases both HPLOUT and HPLCOM at the same mid-rail DC, so there is zero DC voltage across the earpiece. The signal swings differentially. Datasheet §10 "fully differential stereo headphone output" (Figure 10-20) is the reference topology.
- BTL mode is software-configured at codec init time via the I²C register interface. The Linux `snd_soc_tlv320aic3x` driver handles this through the device tree + ALSA mixer controls. If the driver does not automatically select BTL, we configure it via ALSA mixer commands during bring-up.

The right channel (HPROUT pin 23, HPRCOM pin 22) is unused — explicit `no_connect` flags on the schematic.

### 5.3 Reset topology

- **R_RESET:** 10 kΩ 0402, from +3V3 to RESET pin (pin 31). Holds RESET high when the Pi GPIO is tristated (at boot, before the dtoverlay binds).
- **C_RESET:** 1 nF 0402 X7R, from RESET pin to AGND. ESD protection + tiny RC filter (~10 µs corner) to suppress edge glitches. Datasheet does not mandate this but the Teensy reference uses it and it is standard practice.
- **CODEC_RESET** sheet port connects directly to pin 31. The Pi drives this low for ≥10 ms at boot to pulse-reset the codec, then releases it. The `simple-audio-card` binding in the device tree handles the assertion/release automatically when the driver probes.

### 5.4 Unused analog inputs — per-pin termination

Four analog input pins are unused in our mono-mic configuration. Each gets its own 100 nF coupling cap to AGND (NOT bussed onto a shared cap — that was a defect in the previous rev and would create undefined mux behavior).

- **C_MIC1RP:** 100 nF 0402, between pin 12 (MIC1RP) and AGND
- **C_MIC1RM:** 100 nF 0402, between pin 13 (MIC1RM) and AGND
- **C_MIC2L:** 100 nF 0402, between pin 14 (MIC2L/LINE2L/MICDET) and AGND
- **C_MIC2R:** 100 nF 0402, between pin 16 (MIC2R/LINE2R) and AGND

Each of these sits on its own dedicated net between the pin and its cap. The `check_codec_subsheet.py` checker enforces this (no more than one IC pin and one cap terminal per unused-input net).

### 5.5 Unused outputs — NC flags

- **HPROUT** (pin 23): `no_connect` flag
- **HPRCOM** (pin 22): `no_connect` flag
- **LEFT_LOP** (pin 27): `no_connect` flag
- **LEFT_LOM** (pin 28): `no_connect` flag
- **RIGHT_LOP** (pin 29): `no_connect` flag
- **RIGHT_LOM** (pin 30): `no_connect` flag

Why no_connect on the line outputs? The prototype's Codec Zero uses its Lineout terminals for the earpiece, but that's a different chip (DA7212) with a different output stage (including on-HAT class-AB amplification). On our AIC3104, the raw line-output pins are spec'd for ≥10 kΩ loads (datasheet §8.5) — driving a 140 Ω handset would be out of spec. HPLOUT/HPLCOM BTL is rated for ≥16 Ω and gives us ample headroom at 140 Ω.

## 6. Ground handling

- **Single GND net** across the whole sheet — `AGND`, `DGND`, `DRVSS` are all the same net in the schematic.
- **Exposed pad (pin 33)** is tied directly to GND.
- **AGND/DGND partition** is a layout concern addressed in Phase 4 (PCB placement) and Phase 5 (routing), not a schematic concern. The datasheet §13.1 recommends "kept separated" at the copper level — we do that by routing sensitive analog traces (MIC1LP, MIC1LM, MICBIAS) over a continuous ground pour and keeping them far from switching digital traces.

## 8. Software / device-tree integration notes

These are not implemented by Phase 2 but are listed here so Phase 6 has a clear checklist.

### 8.1 Device tree overlay

File: `pi/image/rootfs-overlay/boot/firmware/overlays/digits-codec.dts`

- `compatible = "ti,tlv320aic3104"` — already correct in the existing overlay, no change needed.
- `AVDD-supply`, `IOVDD-supply`, `DRVDD-supply` — all reference `vdd_3v3_reg` (already correct).
- `DVDD-supply` — the existing overlay does not declare this; it should either be omitted (if the AIC3x Linux driver does not require it) or reference a fixed-regulator node for the onboard +1V8 rail. Action for Phase 6: verify driver behavior on a dry boot.
- **NEW fragment needed:** configure GPIO4 as ALT0 (GPCLK0) at 12.288 MHz. The exact dtparam syntax depends on the Pi Zero 2 W's device tree conventions. Candidate: `dtparam=gpclk0=on` or a `pinctrl` fragment + `clk-fixed-factor` provider. Research required in Phase 6.

### 8.2 ALSA mixer controls

File: `pi/digitsd/internal/audio/alsa.go`

- `detectCodec()` already looks for an ALSA card named `digitscodec`, which matches `simple-audio-card,name = "digitscodec"` in the DTS. No change.
- The current mixer control name is `"PCM"`. The `snd_soc_tlv320aic3x` driver exposes controls like `"PCM Playback Volume"`, `"HPL Playback Switch"`, `"Left HPCOM Mixer Bypass Switch"`, etc. A `"PCM"` prefix match in amixer usually resolves to `"PCM Playback Volume"` which is what we want. Action for Phase 6: verify on a live card, adjust if needed.

### 8.3 BTL mode selection

The `snd_soc_tlv320aic3x` driver exposes a control for "HPL Output" or equivalent. The sub-options typically include `"Differential of HPRCOM"` (capless AC-coupled mode) and `"Differential of HPLCOM"` (BTL — what we want for the earpiece). Action for Phase 6: set this via `amixer -c digitscodec set '...' ...` in a systemd one-shot at boot.

## 9. Verification invariants (for `check_codec_subsheet.py` in Task 2.3)

The checker will parse `codec.kicad_sch` and assert all of the following. Any failure is a hard fail.

1. **Exactly 13 sheet ports**, matching the names in §3.
2. **No power rail other than +3V3 and GND** crosses the sheet boundary. The +1V8 / DVDD net is entirely internal.
3. **Every power pin has a close-in 100 nF cap:** IOVDD pin 7, DRVDD pin 18, DRVDD pin 24, AVDD pin 25, DVDD pin 32.
4. **DVDD (pin 32) is driven by an LDO**, not by +3V3 directly. The DVDD net has exactly: U1 pin 32, the LDO output, and ≤ 3 capacitors (close-in 100 nF + close-in 1 µF + LDO bulk 10 µF), nothing else.
5. **MICBIAS (pin 15) has a 100 nF bypass to GND**.
6. **Unused analog inputs (MIC1RP, MIC1RM, MIC2L, MIC2R) are individually terminated**: each on its own net, each net has exactly one IC pin and exactly one capacitor. No shared bus.
7. **MIC1LM (pin 11) is NOT on the GND net.** It must be AC-coupled via its own cap to AGND, not DC-shorted.
8. **HPLOUT (pin 19) and HPLCOM (pin 20) connect to sheet ports EAR_P and EAR_N with no DC-blocking caps in the path.**
9. **HPROUT, HPRCOM, LEFT_LOP, LEFT_LOM, RIGHT_LOP, RIGHT_LOM are marked no_connect** — not just floating, explicit NC flag.
10. **Exposed pad (pin 33)** is on the GND net.
11. **`check_codec_symbol.py` still passes** (the library symbol did not regress).
