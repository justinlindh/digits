Getting Started: Plugging In _▢✕
01 Plugging in & powering onGet the phone booted and ready Setup

It's a real telephone. It makes real calls. That's it. Before your first call you'll plug the phone in, join it to your Wi-Fi once, and pair it with your account.

What you'll need

Wi-FiA 2.4 GHz network with internet. The phone does not support 5 GHz-only networks.
A phone or laptopFor the one-time setup, and to pair the phone afterwards.
Your accountSign in to the Digits app at app.digits.family to pair the phone and choose its number.

Plug it in

  1. Place the phone on a flat surface with the handset resting on the cradle.
  2. Plug the power adapter into the port on the back of the phone.
  3. Plug the other end into a wall outlet.
  4. The red LED on the front blinks briefly as the phone boots.

The status LED sits to the
left of the keypad.

i First boot takes 30–60 seconds. The phone is starting its internal computer. You'll know it's ready when the LED goes dark (idle, on-hook) and you hear a faint click from the ringer.

The LED while it starts up

For the first ~10 seconds the light shows the phone's last known state. In the strips below, = lit, = dark; left → right is time.

LED patternMeaning
Smooth breathingPaired and working normally
Brief flash every 1.7 sUnpaired: waiting for a pairing code
Two quick flashes, pauseIn Wi-Fi setup mode
Rapid blinkingRecovery mode or error
OffFirst boot (brand-new device)

Getting Started: Wi-Fi Setup _▢✕
02 Wi-Fi setup & pairingTell the phone your network: you only do this once Setup

Setup has two parts: first you join the phone to your Wi-Fi, then you pair it from the Digits app and pick its number. You only do this once.

Step 1: Join the phone's hotspot

During setup your phone broadcasts its own temporary network. On your phone or laptop, join:

Digits-XXXX (no password needed)

The XXXX is printed on the bottom of your phone. Most devices pop a “Sign in to network” notification: tap it. If not, open a browser to http://192.168.4.1.

Step 2: Enter your Wi-Fi details

The setup page loads automatically. Pick your home network, type its password, then tap Connect. That's all this page asks for.

Step 3: Wait for the reboot

The phone saves and restarts (~30 s). Reconnect your own device to your normal Wi-Fi. Your Digits phone is now on your network.

Step 4: Pair the phone & pick your number

Now that it's online, the phone needs pairing to your account. Lift the handset and the phone reads out a 6-digit pairing code aloud (it repeats, and a fresh code is issued every few minutes). Then, on your phone or laptop:

  1. Open the Digits app at app.digits.family and sign in.
  2. Go to Lines, then Add a phone, and type the 6-digit code the handset read out.
  3. Choose your line number (format XXX-XXXX), name the handset, then tap Pair.
✓ Setup is complete once it's paired: pick up the handset and you'll hear a dial tone, a steady hum. That's the phone telling you it's online and ready.

Using Your Phone: Calls _▢✕
03 Making & receiving callsThe whole point of the thing Calls

Make a call

  1. Pick up the handset: lift it off the cradle.
  2. Listen for the dial tone: a steady hum means ready.
  3. Dial the 7-digit number. e.g. 271-0001 → dial 2710001.
  4. Wait: you'll hear ringing while their phone rings.
  5. Talk! When they pick up, you're connected.
i No apps, no logins, no hold music. Just a phone call.

Answer a call

  1. The bell rings: a real mechanical bell.
  2. Pick up the handset. That's it: you're connected.

Hang up

  1. Place the handset back on the cradle. The call ends immediately.
! Don't leave the handset off the cradle. The other person hears silence, and your phone can't receive new calls.

What the tones mean

SoundMeaning
Steady humDial tone: ready to dial
Ringing (brrring… brrring…)Their phone is ringing
Busy signal (fast beeping)They're already on a call
Silence / error toneCall couldn't connect: hang up and try again

Using Your Phone: Service Codes _▢✕
04 Service codes & call returnShortcuts you dial while off-hook Settings

Service codes are hidden shortcuts. Lift the handset, dial the code like a phone number, and the phone responds with a voice prompt or a beep.

Volume

CodeLevel
*#*0Mute
*#*1Quiet
*#*5Medium
*#*9Maximum

Dial *#* then any digit 0–9. Saved across reboots.

How the phone answers

Double beepVolume changed; also the start & end of the audio test
Spoken promptSetup, re-pair, and factory reset ask you to press * to confirm
SilentReboot and shutdown make no beep; watch the LED instead
i The letter codes are mnemonics: SETUP=73887, TEST=8378 on the keypad.

Other codes

CodeAlsoWhat it does
*#8378#*#TEST#Audio test: records about 5 seconds and plays it back. Checks mic & earpiece.
*#73887#*#SETUP#Wi-Fi setup: reboots into setup mode (press * to confirm). Use it when your network changes.
*##*–Reboot: restarts the phone (about a minute).
*#*#–Safe shutdown: powers off gracefully. Unplug after the LED goes dark.

Call return: *69 & *89

Dialed straight from dial tone (no *# prefix), just like a 1990s phone line.

  1. Pick up and dial *69. A voice says: “The last call you received was from [number].”
  2. Press 1 to call them back, or hang up to cancel.
  3. If their line is busy, the phone keeps trying for 30 minutes and rings you with a short-short-long pattern when it's free: pick up and you're connected automatically.
  4. To cancel a pending retry, pick up and dial *89.

Using Your Phone: Voicemail _▢✕
05 VoicemailA built-in answering machine Messages

When a call rings unanswered past the timeout, the phone picks up, plays your greeting, and records a message. Your handset mic stays muted while it records, so a caller never hears your room.

● Message-waiting light. When the phone is idle and you have an unheard message, the front LED gives a slow pulse instead of staying dark. A dark LED means no new messages.

Listen: *98

Lift the handset and dial *98. The phone says how many new messages you have, then plays them oldest first. While a message plays:

KeyAction
7Delete & go to next (“Message deleted”)
9Save & go to next (“Message saved”)
#Skip: stays new, you'll hear it again
*Replay from the start

Letting a message finish marks it heard and keeps it. Only 7 deletes for good. Saved messages play after the new ones.

Your greeting

CodeWhat it does
*96Audition: hear your current greeting as callers hear it.
*97Record: speak after the tone, press # when done (“Greeting saved”). Up to 60 s.
*99Delete: remove your greeting, restore the standard one.
i Pick up while the greeting or recording plays and the phone hands you the live call: the machine steps aside.

Good to know

StorageKeeps up to 50 messages. When full, the oldest is deleted to make room: clear messages regularly.
Message lengthEnds when the caller hangs up (10-minute backstop cap).
Turning it on/offPer line in the web app, along with the ring timeout (5–60 s before it picks up).

Using Your Phone: Party Line _▢✕
06 Party line: three-way callingClassic ’90s residential three-way 3-way

Add a third person to a call the old-fashioned way: with a “flash.” A flash is a quick press-and-release of the hook switch (the cradle button), 100–600 ms on-hook. Too short does nothing; too long hangs up.

How to do it

GestureWhat happens
Flash during an active callThe person you're talking to drops to a silent hold; you hear a second dial tone
Dial a third numberYou hear ringback; their phone rings
Flash again after they answerAll three are merged into one conversation
Flash before they answerDrops the attempt; returns you to the held person
Anyone hangs up (host or guest)The whole party line ends for all three; there is no drop back to a two-person call
i Hard-capped at three people, matching residential three-way calling on the old telephone switches.
🔒 Still fully encrypted. A party-line call runs as three independent encrypted connections, mixed locally on each phone. The server never hears the audio: even with three people on the line.

Practising the flash

It takes a moment to get the timing. Press the cradle button down and let it pop back up in well under a second: like tapping it. If the call ends, you held it too long. If nothing happens, you were too quick.

Reference: Status Light & Privacy _▢✕
07 The status light & your privacyWhat the LED means, and what we never store Reference

LED indicator: normal operation

Once booted, the small red light shows what the phone is doing right now. Read each strip left → right as the blink pattern over time:

LED patternMeaning
OffIdle, on-hook: ready to receive calls
Solid onHandset off the cradle (dial tone or dialing)
Smooth breathingActive call in progress
Blinking (~1/second)Incoming call: pick up to answer
Slow pulseYou have an unheard voicemail
Brief flash every 1.7 sUnpaired: pick up to hear your pairing code
Rapid blinkingSomething went wrong: try rebooting

Privacy: built in, not bolted on

End-to-end encrypted calls. Voice is encrypted directly between the two phones. Not even the Digits network can listen.

No call recordings. Live calls are never recorded or stored. When a call ends, it's gone. (If you turn on voicemail, messages are saved only on your own phone, never on our servers.)

Hardware mic disconnect. When the handset rests on the cradle, the microphone is physically disconnected: an electrical cut, not a software mute. A phone on the hook cannot listen.

✓ What we store: your account, household, line assignments, and call metadata (who, when, how long).
✕ What we never store: voice audio, recordings, transcripts, or location. There is no mechanism in the server to capture call audio.

Help: Troubleshooting _▢✕
08 TroubleshootingWhen something isn't right Help
No dial tone when I pick upWait 60 s: it may still be booting. Check the power adapter (both ends) and that your Wi-Fi works. If the LED blinks rapidly and won't stop, reboot: dial *##* and wait ~60 s.
No dial tone after setupThe Wi-Fi password may be wrong. Dial *#73887# to re-enter setup. Make sure it's a 2.4 GHz network: 5 GHz-only won't work.
Can't join the Digits-XXXX hotspotThe hotspot is only on in setup mode. If already set up, dial *#73887# first. On some devices, “forget” old networks or toggle Wi-Fi off/on.
Setup page didn't loadWait 10–15 s after joining for the captive-portal prompt. Otherwise open a browser to http://192.168.4.1. Confirm you're on Digits-XXXX, not your home network.
Bell rings but no one's thereThe caller likely hung up before you answered. Confirm your number with whoever's calling.
Call sounds bad / echoeySet a lower volume level (try *#*3; *#*0–*#*9 set the level directly). Run the audio test (*#8378#) and listen back. Move the phone away from the Wi-Fi router if they're touching.
Not ringing for incoming callsMake sure the handset is fully seated on the cradle: slightly off and the phone thinks you're using it. The idle LED should be off.
Need to change Wi-Fi networkDial *#73887# (*#SETUP#) while off-hook. The phone reboots into setup mode; follow the Wi-Fi steps on page 3.
i Still stuck? Visit digits.family or email support@digits.family. Have your phone number and pairing code handy.
